## About Orbit

**Orbit is a startup focused on building a growing ecosystem of digital products.**

Our mission is to turn ideas into meaningful products that simplify everyday tasks, improve productivity, and make technology more useful and accessible.

We believe that great ideas can become great products when creativity meets engineering. Orbit brings multiple products together under one vision, with each product designed to serve a distinct purpose while contributing to a larger ecosystem.

## Our Vision

To build an ecosystem of innovative, reliable, and user-focused products that create meaningful impact and grow with the people who use them.

## What We Do

Orbit is the home of multiple products across different areas of technology.

- **Innovation** — Exploring new ideas and turning them into practical solutions.
- **Product Development** — Designing and building digital experiences.
- **Technology** — Using modern tools and engineering to solve real problems.
- **Continuous Improvement** — Evolving our products through learning, experimentation, and feedback.

As Orbit grows, this space will showcase our products, projects, and the ideas we're bringing to life.

## Our Principles

- **Think Boldly** — Challenge limitations and explore new possibilities.
- **Build With Purpose** — Focus on real problems and meaningful solutions.
- **Keep It Simple** — Make products intuitive, useful, and accessible.
- **Stay Curious** — Learn, experiment, and improve continuously.
- **Grow Together** — Build an ecosystem where every product contributes to a bigger vision.

## The Orbit Ecosystem
